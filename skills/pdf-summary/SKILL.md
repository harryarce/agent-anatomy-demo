# PDF Summary Skill

Purpose: Generate a short escalation summary suitable for attaching to a ticket.

Workflow:
- Read local policy and order evidence.
- Produce bullets: evidence, decision, action owner.
- Keep under 120 words.

Example:
Input: refund policy text + order delay facts
Output:
- Evidence: clause and order lookup
- Decision: approve or deny with reason
- Owner: finance queue or support follow-up
